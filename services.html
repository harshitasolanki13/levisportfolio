What I Offer

Design
Services

Each engagement is tailored to the specific demands of the project — from a single room to a complete architectural interior.

01
Space Planning
Where proportion meets purpose

The foundation of any interior — the considered organisation of space to serve how people actually live, move, and gather. I work at every scale, from a studio apartment to a multi-level residence, always guided by natural light, human scale, and the rhythms of daily life.

  • Architectural space analysis
  • Furniture layout and flow planning
  • Light choreography and window placement
  • Zoning for function and experience
  • Technical drawings and CAD documentation
02
Interior Styling
The edit that makes everything cohere

For spaces that are architecturally complete but feel unfinished — or simply need a considered refresh. Interior styling is the art of the final layer: the placement of objects, the layering of textiles, the selection of art, and the curation of surfaces that brings a room to life.

  • Furniture arrangement and room composition
  • Soft furnishings — cushions, throws, rugs
  • Object curation and display
  • Art selection and placement
  • Plant and greenery styling
03
FF&E Procurement
The finest materials, sourced with intention

I have cultivated relationships with makers, artisans, and ateliers across India and internationally — allowing me to source furniture, fixtures, and equipment that is appropriate in both character and quality. Every piece I specify is selected to serve the vision, not simply fill the space.

  • Furniture specification and sourcing
  • Lighting design and procurement
  • Custom furniture commissioning
  • Fabric and materials library curation
  • Import, customs, and logistics management
04
3D Visualisation
Inhabit the space before it exists

Photorealistic rendering allows clients to see — truly see — how a space will look and feel before a single decision is locked. My visualisation process is integrated into the design process, not an afterthought, allowing ideas to be tested and refined in three dimensions at every stage.

  • Concept moodboards and material boards
  • 3D modelling of designed spaces
  • Photorealistic renders — day and night
  • Walkthrough animations
  • VR-ready models (on request)
05
Project Management
Protecting the vision through to delivery

A beautifully designed space can be undone by poor execution. I offer full site supervision and contractor coordination for projects where complete oversight is required — managing timelines, quality control, supplier relationships, and client communication throughout the build.

  • Contractor briefing and management
  • Site visits and quality inspection
  • Timeline and budget tracking
  • Punch-list and snagging
  • Final installation and styling
How We Work

The Process

01
Discovery

A conversation — about how you live, what you love, and what you want your space to feel like. No brief, no fee.

02
Concept

Moodboards, spatial concept, material direction, and initial space planning — presented as a coherent design narrative.

03
Design

Full design development: FF&E specification, technical drawings, 3D visualisation, and detailed material selection.

04
Execution

Procurement, site management, contractor coordination, and quality control through every stage of the build.

05
Completion

Final installation, styling, and photography. Your space — fully realised, ready to inhabit.
