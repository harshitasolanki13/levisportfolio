The Ivory

A private family residence in South Delhi — designed as a sanctuary of restrained luxury, where every surface, texture, and threshold has been considered with quiet intention.

The brief called for warmth without excess. The clients, a young family with a deep appreciation for craft and travel, wanted their home to feel like a distilled version of their experiences — grounded in the Indian subcontinent yet fluent in a broader, international design language.

Warm travertine flows through the living spaces as both floor and wall cladding, anchoring the palette in earth and mineral. Against this, we layered hand-knotted wool rugs in ochre and ivory, hand-stitched linen upholstery, and furniture in unlacquered brass and fumed oak.

The main living room — travertine walls, fumed oak cabinetry, unlacquered brass.

Left: Dining room with custom brass pendant and hand-thrown ceramic tableware. Right: Primary bedroom in linen and bleached oak.

Light was the fifth material in this project. Working with the home's north-facing garden, we choreographed openings to pull in soft, diffused daylight — avoiding the harsh directional light that can strip warmth from neutral interiors.

The kitchen is a study in function elevated to beauty — Calacatta marble slabs, custom cabinetry in deep sage, and open shelving in solid walnut carrying hand-crafted vessels collected from across Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu.

Kitchen with Calacatta marble, sage cabinetry, and curated ceramic collection.

The study — a quieter corner of the home in aged leather, dark walnut, and manuscript-inspired wall art.
