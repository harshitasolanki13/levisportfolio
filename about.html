Story

Levisha Malviya is a luxury interior designer whose practice spans private residences, boutique hospitality, and thoughtfully curated commercial spaces across India and internationally.

Born in Jaipur and educated in design at the National Institute of Design (NID) and the Politecnico di Milano, Levisha brings an uncommon depth to the craft — one shaped by an equal appreciation for Mughal symmetry, Brutalist materiality, and the silent luxury of Scandinavian restraint.

Her studio, founded in New Delhi in 2012, operates as a collaborative atelier — small by intention, rigorous by design. Each project is approached as a long-term relationship, with Levisha personally involved from initial concept through to installation.

Her work has been featured in Architectural Digest India, Elle Decor, and The Hindu Weekend, and she has been recognised by the Interior Design Society of India and the Asia Pacific Interior Design Awards.

When not in her studio or on site, she is most likely at an auction house, a ceramics market, or on a slow walk through a city she's never seen before — collecting the material for what comes next.
